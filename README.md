## Overview

**ASTRA** is a modern astrological guidance platform tailored for real-world Indian dilemmas:
- **Career Decisions**: Job switches, promotion timing, salary hikes, business stability.
- **Relationships & Marriage**: Compatibility, marriage timing, family dynamics without shame or judgment.
- **Financial Cycles**: Wealth timing, investments, relocation, and higher studies abroad.

---

## Key Features

1. **3D Scroll Hero Journey**: Apple-style smooth interactive scroll canvas scrubbing 60 WebP frames showcasing hand-to-kundli transformation with an authentic iPhone frosted glass CTA.
2. **Interactive Rashi Transit Explorer**: 12-Rashi responsive selector with a dedicated Pop-up Modal delivering today's planetary transit guidance, 3 focused insight meters (Mind & Focus, Best Timing, Stress Level), and simple Satvik Vedic habits.
3. **Satvik Practical Remedies**: Zero-fear, daily natural routines (Sunlight, Pranayama, Jiva Seva, Calm Workspace) that promote daily peace without costly rituals.
4. **Astrologer Audio Intros**: Verified astrologer profiles (Dr. Saurabh Kulkarni, Ananya Vashistha, Raghavendra Mishra) with 15-second conversational audio introductions.
5. **Bilingual Experience**: Instant 1-click toggle between everyday Indian English and natural spoken Hindi.
6. **Pure Architectural Square Geometry**: Signature Liquid Silver Chrome & Platinum aesthetic with strict `border-radius: 0` design system.

---

## Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend**: Vanilla HTML5, Modern CSS3 (Liquid Silver design system, Backdrop Filter Glassmorphism), Vanilla JavaScript ES6+.
- **Modular Data Layer**:
  - `data/i18n.js`: English & Hindi UI translations
  - `data/roadmaps.js`: Sample 3-Point Kundli answers
  - `data/rashis.js`: 12-Rashi transits, audio advice & remedy data
- **Local Server**: Node.js static server (`server.js`) on Port 3000.
